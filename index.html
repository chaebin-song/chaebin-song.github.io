I am a Ph.D. student in the Hydraulic Engineering Laboratory at Changwon National University. My research focuses on air–water two-phase flow in pipes, with applications to urban drainage systems.

In the laboratory, I characterize two-phase flow using image-based measurement and analysis, including PIV and spectral analysis of flow fields. I also model it numerically using CFD. Beyond two-phase flow, I am studying the fundamentals of turbulence and its analysis. Through funded research projects, I carry out field surveys covering flood safety assessment of river facilities, monitoring of sediment and mixing processes in rivers and lakes, and LSPIV-based surface velocity and discharge measurement.
